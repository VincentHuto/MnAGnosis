# Stage 16 — Integration and Release

## Purpose

Balance, profile, validate, and document the complete capstone without adding
new encounter systems.

## Balance Targets

- Prepared solo first clear: 12–15 minutes.
- Practiced projection rematch: 8–10 minutes.
- Solo patterns: no more than two simultaneous high-attention responses.
- Every damaging Edict: at least 1.25 seconds of readable warning.
- Ordinary spells and weapons: steady useful damage throughout.
- Authored counters: required transitions and major damage advantages.
- Party scaling: health, overlap, and recovery changes without mandatory roles.

## Accessibility

Every required cue must combine:

- Distinct geometry or motion.
- Subtitle or concise text declaration.
- Distinct audio.
- A non-color discriminator such as outline, shape, rhythm, or position.

Reduced-particle mode retains safe paths, active targets, celestial allegiance,
contradiction segments, delayed-event traces, and final eclipse timing. Camera
movement remains optional; no required solution depends on screen shake.

## Performance

Profile:

- Multipart transform and collision cost.
- Celestial beams and omitted-sector updates.
- Concurrent particles and post effects.
- Projection static and discontinuous animation.
- Two simultaneous arena cells on a representative dedicated server.
- Low-end client behavior at minimum supported render distance.

Performance reductions may lower cosmetic density but cannot remove combat
information.

## Verification Matrix

Run complete original and projection encounters:

- Solo.
- Two players.
- Four players.
- One participant death.
- Owner death with survivors.
- Disconnect within and beyond grace.
- Server restart in every phase.
- Dimension unload.
- Wipe during each authored sentence.
- Inventory-full reward delivery.
- All Thesis parts already learned.
- Reduced particles and subtitles enabled.

Verify arena restoration hashes, empty delayed-event ledgers, no transient
entities, idempotent reward receipts, correct first-clear state, and reusable
cells after every terminal case.

## Release Boundaries

Reject release changes that add Tier 7, a Seven Heavens gauntlet, escalating
rematch tiers, generic Exception grinding, mandatory party roles, or the full
Original Work editor. Those require independent approved designs.

## Completion Criteria

The feature is ready only when the complete validation matrix passes, no
blocking client or dedicated-server errors remain, all required localization
exists, and the lore bible and canonical master match shipped behavior.
