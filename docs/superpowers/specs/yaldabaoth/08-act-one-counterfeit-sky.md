# Stage 08 — Act I: The Counterfeit Sky

## Purpose

Deliver the complete celestial opening, teach all three encounter verbs, and
transition from Yaldabaoth's distant interference to his bodily entrance.

## Opening State

Sun and Moon orbit in opposite directions above the seven-ring arena.
Yaldabaoth is visible at impossible scale beyond the boundary. Only the head,
passing coils, and tail may enter the playable space.

Hostile celestial attacks alternate at low intensity, then overlap as anti-focus
or party scaling increases. Yaldabaoth adds tail sweeps and reversed-knockback
roars without becoming a damage target.

## Exception Tutorial

Applicable subjects advertise Relation, Definition, or Continuance with
distinct symbols, sounds, and subtitles. Invalid actions give immediate
feedback and consume neither cooldown nor attempt state.

The required resolution is:

1. Stagger both celestials within the allowed synchronization window.
2. Use Relation to link Sun and Moon.
3. Use Definition to permit the Sun's beam to affect its linked counterpart.
4. Use Continuance to capture the declared beam.
5. Release it through their orbital crossing.

The order is fixed for the first clear so the encounter can teach its grammar.
Prompts become less explicit on projection rematches.

## Resolution

The eclipse:

- Clears unresolved celestial attacks.
- Breaks both stagger states into dormant sigils.
- Records the tutorial sentence as completed.
- Opens the firmament boundary.
- Moves the controller to `CLAIMS`.

Yaldabaoth enters and declares:

> WHAT I HAVE MADE CANNOT EXCEED ME.

## Scaling and Failure

Solo play never requires simultaneous input. Party play may shorten the
stagger synchronization window and add attack overlap but uses the same
solution. A failed eclipse returns celestials to a recoverable combat state; it
does not reset the entire encounter unless all participants die.

## Verification

- Complete the act solo and with two and four participants.
- Confirm target hints, invalid-action feedback, and fixed first-clear order.
- Confirm anti-focus cannot deadlock stagger synchronization.
- Confirm the eclipse is idempotent and cannot advance twice.
- Confirm dormant celestials survive save/reload and restoration.
