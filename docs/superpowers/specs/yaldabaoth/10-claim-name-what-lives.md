# Stage 10 — Claim: I Name What Lives

## Purpose

Deliver the Definition-focused portion of the main duel. Yaldabaoth attempts
to reduce the ascended player to categories already overcome.

## Combat Flow

This Claim enters near 70–40% boss health.

Yaldabaoth applies one temporary imposed identity:

- Council subject.
- Demon.
- Undead.
- Fae plaything.
- Divine supplicant.

Each identity calls a clearly recognizable spectral echo from its Authority:
structured Council patterns, demonic force, undead persistence, fae
misdirection, or Odin's divine judgment. These are visual and mechanical
callbacks, not complete copies of the original bosses.

Several projected lion heads then appear. Definition allows the player to
declare themselves **Unwritten**, rejecting only the active imposed identity.
While Unwritten:

- The associated Authority echo loses its claim on that player.
- The real lion head becomes distinguishable through stable geometry and
  sound.
- Direct head damage becomes available for a bounded window.

## Limits of Unwritten

Unwritten does not remove species, faction, ownership, collision, damage,
advancement eligibility, or unrelated targeting. It exists only inside the
active Claim and ends at the damage-window boundary.

## Party Scaling

Participants may receive different imposed identities, but every identity can
be rejected independently. One player's Definition action cannot cleanse or
invalidate another player's proof.

## Verification

- Every identity maps to the correct echo and subtitle.
- Identity state is removed on window end, death, logout, wipe, and recovery.
- Unwritten bypasses only the active Claim's targeting.
- Projected heads cannot receive boss damage.
- The real head remains identifiable without color.
- Authority callbacks do not grant their original boss loot or advancements.

## Handoff

Shares its imposed-identity and projection state with the overlap controller in
[Stage 12](12-claims-and-crown-transition.md).
