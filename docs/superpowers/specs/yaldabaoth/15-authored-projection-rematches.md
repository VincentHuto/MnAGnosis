# Stage 15 — Authored Projection Rematches

## Purpose

Make Yaldabaoth repeatable without undoing the first victory. Every rematch is
a practice emanation deliberately authored by the victorious player.

## Entry and Identity

After first clear, the Writ interaction changes from **Enter the First
Firmament** to **Reconstruct the First Error**. The encounter controller
creates `EncounterVariant.PROJECTION`.

The projection follows the same three-act mechanic contract and remains a
serious boss. Its fiction and presentation never imply that the original
authority returned.

## Projection Presentation

- Entire form uses black, white, and severe static.
- Body slices may be absent for individual frames.
- Surfaces invert or exchange inside and outside.
- Segment seams reveal that the form is being projected.
- Animations occasionally reach poses without traversing intervening frames.
- Dialogue describes reconstruction, rehearsal, error, or measurement rather
  than sole divinity.
- Final collapse resembles a dismissed diagram.

The static version must remain readable under reduced effects and must not use
random rainbow glitch language.

## Mechanical Differences

Rematches preserve solutions and accessibility contracts. Tutorial prompts are
reduced, introductory recovery may be shorter, and attack scheduling targets
the 8–10 minute practiced duration. V1 does not add escalating difficulty
tiers or new required solutions.

## Rewards

Eligible participants receive:

- One Ineffable Thesis.
- 64–128 Marks of No Authority.
- Normal rare chances for projection cosmetics and trophies.

They never receive another first-clear Husk, guaranteed trophy, Original Work
unlock, or original-victory presentation.

## Verification

- First-clear state always selects projection on later attempts.
- Variant sync controls assets, dialogue, timing, loot, and revocation.
- Projection failure preserves the Writ and first-clear state.
- Original-only rewards remain impossible through reconnect, commands, or
  simultaneous encounters.
- Reduced prompts do not make the required sentence order ambiguous.
