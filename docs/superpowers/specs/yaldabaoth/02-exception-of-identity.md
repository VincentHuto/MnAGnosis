# Stage 02 — Exception of Identity

## Purpose

Design and deliver the Definition mastery ordeal that proves the player can
reject an imposed category without erasing the meaningful identity beneath it.
Its progression output is the **Exception of Identity**.

## Player Promise

The player survives being misclassified, identifies which properties are
imposed rather than essential, and restores a coherent subject. The ordeal
prepares the exact vocabulary later used when Yaldabaoth names the player.

## Required Design

- Entry requires the Manuscript of Definition at Authorship.
- The ordeal occurs in a bounded Autogenic location with restorable entity
  state.
- At least one subject receives an incorrect but mechanically meaningful name,
  allegiance, species, or function.
- The player must remove the false definition, preserve the subject, and
  restore a valid identity through action rather than a dialogue answer.
- The temporary **Unwritten** state means "not governed by the current imposed
  definition." It does not make the player universally untargetable,
  factionless, or immune to consequences.
- Failure cannot permanently corrupt entity data, faction state, equipment
  rules, or player identity.
- Multiplayer assistance cannot grant the owner's Exception to spectators.

## Progression Contract

The Exception is unique, owner-bound, recoverable, and atomically consumed by
the Broker exchange.

Granting it should produce:

> A name may describe. It may not possess.

## Design Gate

A focused design pass must choose the location, affected subject, imposed
definitions, proof actions, and restoration sequence. The result must test
Definition rather than disguise a conventional boss kill as an identity
lesson.

## Verification

- Entry and reward require Definition Authorship.
- Temporary category changes are server-authoritative and fully reversible.
- Logout, death, unload, and failure restore the player and all subjects.
- Unwritten cannot bypass unrelated protections or progression checks.
- Duplicate completion does not duplicate the proof.
