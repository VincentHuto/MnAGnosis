# Stage 12 — Claims and Crown Transition

## Purpose

Blend the three learned Claims into one escalating main duel and convert the
dormant celestials into Witnesses without adding a fourth puzzle language.

## Claim Scheduling

- There Is No Outside enters from 100–70% health.
- I Name What Lives enters from 70–40%.
- No Consequence Precedes Me enters from 40–10%.
- Once introduced, a Claim may recur until the crown transition.

The scheduler combines Claims only through compatible pressure pairs. Solo
play never receives more than two simultaneous high-attention responses.
Party scaling adds overlap, secondary targets, and shorter recovery rather
than mandatory roles.

Examples of compatible overlap include:

- A delayed tail consequence crossing folded sectors.
- An Authority echo operating inside coil walls.
- A projected head appearing at the endpoint of a declared spatial path.

The scheduler must reject combinations whose safe solutions conflict.

## Stolen-Fire Crown

At the transition threshold, Yaldabaoth:

1. Cancels ordinary Claim scheduling.
2. Resolves or safely clears outstanding consequences.
3. Withdraws borrowed fire into an invulnerable crown.
4. Suspends direct boss damage.
5. Marks the dormant Sun, Moon, and crown as Exception subjects.

Players construct the final sentence:

1. Relation binds Sun and Moon to the crown.
2. Definition changes their allegiance from creations to Witnesses.
3. Continuance repeats the Act I eclipse.

Successful completion strips the crown, changes both celestial states to
`WITNESS`, and enters `REVOCATION`.

## Failure

Invalid sentence steps give feedback without consuming progress already
validated. A full-party death resets the encounter normally. The crown cannot
be damaged, bypassed, or removed through ordinary attacks.

## Verification

- Health thresholds introduce each Claim once.
- Scheduler validation prevents contradictory safe zones and excess solo
  attention.
- Outstanding ledgers clear safely before the crown sentence.
- Sentence completion is ordered, persistent, and idempotent.
- Celestial allegiance cannot change outside a valid controller transition.

## Handoff

Enters [Stage 13](13-act-three-no-other.md) with both celestials allied and the
crown removed.
