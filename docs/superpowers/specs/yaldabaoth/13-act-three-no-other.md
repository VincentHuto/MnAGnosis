# Stage 13 — Act III: No Other

## Purpose

Deliver the combat payoff, interrupted final Edict, player-authored finishing
blow, and complete revocation presentation.

## Witness Actions

Participants select eligible serpent segments through ordinary attacks or a
controller-approved mark.

- Sun pins the selected segment with a vertical white beam.
- Moon removes that pinned segment's protection with an absolute-black
  crescent cut.
- Overlap exposes the lion head for a short unrestricted damage window.

Witness actions cannot reduce Yaldabaoth below the finishing threshold. They
create vulnerability; participants provide the decisive damage.

## Final Combat

The scheduler recombines already learned coils, Authority echoes, delayed
consequences, and celestial lanes at high tempo. It introduces no new rule or
required verb. Every combination retains the solo two-response limit and the
minimum telegraph duration.

At critical health:

1. Seven heaven-rings begin closing.
2. Yaldabaoth becomes fixed at the arena's center.
3. The Edict appears and is spoken:

   > I AM, AND THERE IS NO OTHER.

4. Sun and Moon align for the final eclipse.
5. The eclipse interrupts the Edict.
6. Yaldabaoth becomes vulnerable to participant damage only.

## Revocation

On valid finishing damage:

- Freeze combat and commit encounter success before presentation begins.
- Unthread the tail and body into black-and-white propositions.
- Remove sound from the final roar while preserving the mouth animation.
- Withdraw the head, then continue its mouth motion briefly without a visible
  source.
- Pass Sun and Moon through one another, remove their outlines, and leave two
  ordinary distant lights.
- Open the arena toward the Unwritten Observatory.

The sequence is skippable only after rewards and safe-return state are
committed. It cannot replay after reconnect.

## Verification

- Witnesses never land the finishing damage.
- The final Edict cannot begin with unresolved lethal hazards.
- Success commits once even if several players strike simultaneously.
- Revocation survives save/reload without duplicating rewards or restarting
  combat.
- Reduced effects preserve ring closure, eclipse, and vulnerability cues.
