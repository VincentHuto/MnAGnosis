# The Three Disciplines: Post-Tier-6 Idea Vault

## Status and purpose

This document is an unrestricted creative reservoir for post-Tier-6 MnAGnosis
content. It organizes possible spells, components, modifiers, Laws, artifacts,
materials, places, beings, progression, and update themes around three distinct
disciplines of authorship.

Nothing in this vault is canon, scheduled, or approved for implementation merely
because it appears here. Individual ideas should be promoted through a focused design
and implementation cycle before entering the roadmap.

The vault extends the established premise:

> The world is not false. It is unfinished.

Its governing distinction is:

> Lower magic changes objects. Gnosis changes the relationships that allow objects,
> lives, and events to mean what they mean.

## Creative direction

The dominant tone is austere metaphysical awe: clean geometry, restrained language,
precise effects, and power that feels inevitable rather than chaotic. Cosmic unease
is strongest when identity is revised. Surreal spectacle is reserved for crossings,
higher-order spaces, and moments when several valid states become visible together.

The three disciplines are not factions or mutually exclusive skill trees. They are
three answers to what it means to author reality. A player may pursue all three, but
each must retain a recognizable play style, visual language, risk, and philosophical
temptation.

# The Three Disciplines

## Architectonics: Authorship of Relation

Architectonics rejects absolute position. A wall, mountain, doorway, and distant point
are arrangements of adjacency, direction, support, boundary, and scale.

Its signature verbs are:

- **Fold:** make separated positions temporarily adjacent.
- **Orient:** redefine down, facing, flow, or support.
- **Assemble:** coerce existing matter into deliberate geometry.
- **Bound:** establish an area in which a rewritten rule applies.
- **Project:** manifest a possible structure without making it materially real.
- **Excise:** remove a region from ordinary spatial relationships.

Gravity Convergence, Gravity Shift, Living Land, the tesseract, Precision, and Polarity
form its earliest vocabulary.

Its magic appears as lines, planes, wireframes, measured arcs, and matter traveling
along unnaturally clean trajectories. Its characteristic risk is **Dislocation**: the
world attempting to reconcile contradictory positions.

Its temptation is to treat the world as inert material.

> Distance is an agreement between points.

## Autogenesis: Authorship of Identity

Autogenesis treats a living thing as a continuing declaration rather than a fixed
object. Species, allegiance, injury, behavior, immunity, and individuality are
properties that can be revised, but a living subject may resist or reinterpret that
revision.

Its signature verbs are:

- **Name:** select which identity or category a spell recognizes.
- **Emanate:** express a being through an alternate temporary form.
- **Recast:** alter species, body plan, scale, or material.
- **Ordain:** give a creature a new purpose or behavioral law.
- **Sever:** remove an immunity, allegiance, ownership, or relationship.
- **Reconcile:** merge compatible traits or restore an authored identity.

True Damage and Banish touch this discipline, but Autogenesis should usually do
something more interesting than direct damage. Its best spells change what combatants
are allowed to be.

Its visual language uses white anatomical diagrams, black silhouettes, branching
symmetry, taxonomic marks, and briefly duplicated bodies that disagree about their
final form. Transformation is precise rather than fleshy. Its characteristic risk is
**Dissonance**: a subject's history pushing back against a revised identity.

Its temptation is to treat beings as editable definitions.

> The body remembers every name it survived.

## Worldlines: Authorship of Continuity

Worldlines rejects the isolated present. Movement, cause, memory, spell history, and
unrealized possibilities form paths that an ascendant can observe and edit.

Its signature verbs are:

- **Record:** preserve a state, path, target, or event.
- **Recall:** return something to a recorded relation.
- **Continue:** extend motion or an effect beyond its apparent ending.
- **Branch:** produce several valid continuations from one event.
- **Recur:** cause an earlier act to happen again under bounded conditions.
- **Conclude:** collapse competing possibilities into one outcome.

Suspension and Exchange already touch this discipline. Causal Bookmark, spell memory,
and Worldline shapes form its central vocabulary.

Its visuals are sparse: ghost paths, delayed outlines, particles marking future
positions, sounds arriving before impacts, and brief views of states that never became
actual. Its characteristic risk is **Remainder**: a discarded outcome that still
demands resolution.

Its temptation is to treat consequences as optional branches.

> An event does not vanish merely because it has finished.

## Intersections

| Intersection | Domain | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Architectonics + Autogenesis | Living structure | Grow a guardian from nearby terrain |
| Architectonics + Worldlines | Impossible travel | Walk a recorded route regardless of present distance |
| Autogenesis + Worldlines | Persistent identity | Recall an earlier valid form without restoring health |
| All three | Full authorship | Define a space, its inhabitants, and how events recur within it |

# Spell and Component Vault

## Architectonic components

### Foundation

- **Reassembled Land:** draw nearby blocks into a bridge, wall, stair, pillar, dome,
  or saved template while conserving matter. Precision selects real assembly or a
  nonmaterial preview.
- **Axial Ordination:** align affected entities, projectiles, falling blocks, or spell
  manifestations with a chosen axis or plane.
- **Boundary Condition:** declare that subsequent effects operate inside, outside, or
  across the surface of a visible volume.
- **Load-Bearing Principle:** redirect force, falling impact, or structural damage
  into nearby terrain.
- **Lattice Emanation:** create a temporary scaffold along whose edges and vertices
  other spell effects may travel or repeat.
- **Metric Compression:** reduce effective travel distance within a bounded corridor
  without changing its visible length.

### Frontier

- **Axiom of Adjacency:** make two surfaces temporarily touching. Interpretations may
  selectively transmit entities, projectiles, fluids, light, sound, or spells.
- **Euclidean Refusal:** create a plane that redirects straight traversal through
  reflection, rotation, lateral displacement, or return.
- **Hollow Domain:** omit a volume from collision and interaction without destroying
  its contents. Omitted matter remains visible as black wireframe absence.
- **Coordinate Transposition:** exchange two bounded regions block-for-block while
  applying strict block-entity and permission rules.
- **Unbounded Interior:** author a container or room whose interior exceeds its
  exterior bounds.

### Transcendent work: The World Seam

The caster defines a plane whose two sides cease to be connected. Entities, redstone,
fluids, and explosions cannot cross it. Polarity instead stitches two distant planes
together. Closure requires restoring the original relation or reconciling everything
that crossed.

## Autogenic components

### Foundation

- **Axiom of Harm:** remove one relevant immunity while preserving the original damage
  type and attribution.
- **Ordain Instinct:** grant a simple behavior such as defend, pursue, flee, gather,
  orbit, imitate, remain still, or target a named category.
- **Predatory Principle:** give peaceful creatures coordinated hostile behavior based
  on a declared hunting rule.
- **Emanate Creature:** express a reusable creature pattern as an avatar, swarm,
  guardian, projectile-creature, or temporary body.
- **Redaction of Trait:** temporarily remove a compatible property such as flight,
  immunity, ownership, allegiance, ranged attack, armor, or target recognition.
- **Authored Succession:** declare what a creature becomes or releases when it dies.

### Frontier

- **Taxonomic Error:** make an entity count as a different category for targeting and
  AI decisions without changing its visible form.
- **Name the Many:** treat several subjects as one distributed identity and divide
  selected effects among them.
- **Consensus Body:** construct a temporary body from traits contributed by nearby
  creatures, each of which retains a claim on its contribution.
- **True Self:** create an autonomous manifestation of the caster that chooses spells,
  positioning, and tactics according to a declared purpose.
- **Revision of Scale:** change the scale at which a subject collides and interacts,
  not merely its rendered size or statistics.
- **Anatomy of Stone:** give a structure temporary organs, senses, joints, purpose,
  and behavior while preserving its material form.

### Transcendent work: The Second Definition

A target receives two simultaneous valid identities. The caster may select which
definition governs supported interactions, but every disagreement creates a
Contradiction. Closure chooses one identity and resolves the consequences attributed
to the rejected definition.

## Worldline components

### Foundation

- **Causal Bookmark:** record position, dimension, facing, velocity, and safe return
  space for a later recall that does not restore health or inventory.
- **Path Memorial:** record a route that can guide entities, projectiles, particles,
  summons, or later components.
- **Deferred Arrival:** remove a traveling subject from the intervening moments while
  preserving its eventual arrival.
- **Continuation:** extend motion or an effect beyond its ordinary conclusion.
- **Causal Relay:** transfer a compatible consequence between subjects while
  preserving attribution.
- **Still Point:** preserve motion inside an area, then resume it after the surrounding
  world has advanced.

### Frontier

- **Recurrence:** repeat one compatible spell resolution when a declared trigger
  occurs.
- **Foregone Path:** reveal several future trajectories and commit a subject to one.
- **Revision of Outcome:** restore one tightly bounded world interaction without
  refunding health, deaths, mana, or arbitrary inventory history.
- **Counterfactual Emanation:** manifest a temporary state an entity could recently
  have occupied.
- **Consequence Without Cause:** produce a recorded result before its action occurs,
  creating an obligation to complete the missing cause.
- **The Long Moment:** expand a single instant into a traversable interval containing
  suspended projectiles, sound planes, and incomplete events.

### Transcendent work: The Unfinished Hour

A bounded region records significant events for a short interval. At its conclusion,
the caster may accept the events, recur the interval while participants retain memory,
or restore the physical state while costs, knowledge, deaths, and Contradictions
remain accounted for. Rejected history becomes visible Remainders.

# Shared Spell Grammar

## Shapes

- **Worldline:** travel along a recorded or newly drawn path.
- **Stamp:** apply a component through a selected geometric template.
- **Orbit:** circle a point, subject, or path.
- **Proxy:** cast from a selected entity, block, link, or emanation.
- **Boundary:** resolve on entry, exit, crossing, or surface contact.
- **Constellation:** join selected points and resolve on vertices, edges, faces, or the
  enclosed volume.
- **Palimpsest:** apply where an earlier compatible spell resolved.
- **Chorus:** resolve through each expression of a shared identity.

## Modifiers

- **Conservation:** borrow, transfer, or rearrange existing matter or energy.
- **Witness:** depend on observation by the caster, target, or another player.
- **Continuity:** preserve selected state through transformation or relocation.
- **Exclusion:** specify what a component must ignore.
- **Multiplicity:** treat one subject as several or several as one.
- **Recursion:** feed a result into one bounded repetition.
- **Symmetry:** mirror across an axis, plane, target, or causal relationship.
- **Threshold:** resolve only when a measurable condition is crossed.
- **Inheritance:** pass a property to a manifestation or successor.
- **Negative Space:** apply everywhere in the shape except the selected region.

## Candidate Authored Laws

- **Adjacency:** change what is spatially connected.
- **Identity:** change which definition governs a subject.
- **Recurrence:** permit an event to resolve more than once.
- **Exclusion:** remove a subject or property from an otherwise universal rule.
- **Continuity:** determine what survives transformation.
- **Measure:** exchange qualitative behavior for a bounded quantity or the reverse.
- **Witness:** make observation part of an effect's validity.
- **Conclusion:** force several unresolved states to become exactly one.

Ordinary components should remain useful post-Tier-6 magic. Law Inscriptions transform
them into dangerous authorship. This preserves Mana and Artifice's spell-building
identity while allowing MnAGnosis to exceed its ordinary grammar.

# Artifact Vault

## Architectonic instruments

- **Unbounded Lattice:** a tesseract-derived focus that records vertices and turns
  completed figures into Boundaries, Constellations, Stamps, or measurements.
- **Plumb of Elsewhere:** indicates the authoritative local direction of gravity and
  previews Gravity Shift fields.
- **Surveyor's Nail:** declares a block a Fixed Point for measurement, rotation,
  preservation, or proxy casting.
- **Compass of Interior Directions:** distinguishes geometrically different exits in
  folded spaces even when they appear identical.
- **Seam-Ripper:** dismantles authored boundaries and closes spatial Contradictions.
- **Atlas of Touching Places:** records authored adjacencies and can reopen one
  expensive connection.
- **Mantle of Corners:** allows brief support on edges and travel around the faces of a
  structure.

## Autogenic instruments

- **Vessel of Names:** stores learned patterns of species, allegiance, behavior, and
  compatible traits.
- **Taxonomist's Needle:** displays the categories by which systems recognize a
  creature and threads one into a spell.
- **Emanation Reliquary:** stores a reusable creature pattern and its permitted
  emanation forms.
- **Negative Crown:** removes selected classifications from the wearer, including the
  benefits that require those classifications.
- **Mask of the Consensus:** links willing creatures for shared senses, effects,
  purpose, or emanation.
- **Pale Anatomy:** displays functional nodes such as locomotion, perception, defense,
  and purpose on creatures and structures.
- **Unclaimed Familiar:** creates a companion whose form is derived from spell, place,
  nearby life, and declared purpose rather than a fixed species.

## Worldline instruments

- **Causal Spindle:** winds recorded paths into threads that can be retraced,
  continued, or shared.
- **Bell After Tolling:** records an event, warns of Closure opportunities, and may be
  heard before it is rung.
- **Pendulum of the Still Point:** reacts to suspended effects, unresolved events, and
  active recording.
- **Compass to the Road Not Taken:** points toward significant unrealized journeys and
  missed branches.
- **Palimpsest Lens:** reveals recent spell resolutions and enables casting from them.
- **Unfinished Hourglass:** holds one bounded deferred consequence outside the normal
  ledger.
- **Shoes of the Returning Step:** safely retrace a short recorded movement sequence.

## Cross-disciplinary masterworks

- **Blank Principle:** acquires exactly one permanent relational purpose through its
  first meaningful use.
- **Broker's Remainder:** pays the metaphysically incomplete fraction of an authored
  exchange, but may belong to an unknown counterparty.
- **Author's Seal:** links authored creations, enables proxy relationships, and closes
  connected works in a safe order when broken.
- **Instrument of Final Measure:** inspects exact scope, ownership, conservation,
  duration, and Closure conditions.
- **Codex Palimpsest:** the blank post-ascension successor to the surrendered Codex,
  written through invention, survival, Closure, and rejection.
- **The Fourth Implement:** represents authored judgment rather than knowledge or
  enacted will and changes appearance according to use.

# Higher-Order Materials

These are consequences and proofs, not conventional ores.

- **Orthogonal Shard:** produced by reconciling incompatible gravity orientations.
- **Fixed Point:** recovered from a collapsed spatial boundary.
- **Shed Name:** left when a creature survives removal of a defining category.
- **Consensus Thread:** created when willing subjects share an effect and survive its
  Closure.
- **Causal Remainder:** residue of a rejected but unerased outcome.
- **Unspent Moment:** earned by releasing suspended events without exploiting them.
- **Witness Glass:** transparent only while not directly observed.
- **Unwritten Ink:** records relationships and conditions but not ordinary prose.
- **White Measure:** exact equivalence recognized by the Broker.
- **Black Measure:** irreducible error left after an apparently exact exchange.
- **Projection Ash:** falls when an emanation or impossible structure is withdrawn.
- **Axiomatic Husk:** remains after the active interpretation of a Law is removed.

# Places and Anomalies

## Architectonic places

- **House of Measures:** a small exterior containing rooms that each demonstrate one
  impossible spatial proposition.
- **Buried Axis:** a colossal line through terrain toward which gravity gradually
  aligns, centered on a missing coordinate.
- **City of One Wall:** a ruin whose floors, roofs, and towers form one continuous
  surface.
- **Revision Scar:** a biome bisected by a plane across which weather, gravity, age,
  or identity disagrees.

## Autogenic places

- **Taxonomy Garden:** many creatures arranged by a hidden classification, each an
  emanation of one subject under a different name.
- **Basilica of the Unnamed:** a severe structure in which names, factions, and
  species-specific targeting cease to function.
- **Orchard of Successors:** trees briefly express what nearby dead creatures might
  have become.
- **Consensus Nest:** architecture built by many species acting as one intelligence,
  with no individual leader.

## Worldline places

- **Road Not Taken:** a path through locations and events the player nearly reached.
- **Observatory of the Unfinished Sky:** displays contradictory celestial states and
  constellations made from recorded journeys.
- **Corridor of One Difference:** repeats one place with exactly one changed rule.
- **Long Moment:** a battle, collapse, storm, or impact expanded into an explorable
  interval.

## The Unwritten Observatory

This higher-order hub has no coordinates. Entry requires a spatial condition, a living
definition, and a completed sequence. Dimensions appear as neighboring surfaces,
builds as geometric sentences, beings as taxonomic diagrams, and events as paths
between stars. It remains sparse and solemn rather than becoming a conventional quest
hub.

# Higher Powers and Minor Beings

## The Measure

An Architectonic Aeon capable of describing the exact relation of everything except
an ascended player. Its encounter classifies the arena into valid and invalid regions.
The player prevails by occupying meaningful but mathematically excluded positions.
The resulting insight is to become **unmeasured**: relational connections can
occasionally matter more than ordinary casting range.

## The Redactor

An Autogenic intelligence that removes properties instead of dealing direct damage.
It removes color, sound, categories, equipment functions, names, and eventually the
distinction between combatant and arena. Players restore each property by proving it
through action. Its insight permits **negative definitions**: explicit statements of
what a subject is not.

## The Interval

A Worldline presence visible only between ticks, animation frames, attacks, or notes.
It can be reached through created pauses, delays, suspended effects, and absences. Its
insight permits a brief **interstitial step** between a declared cause and consequence.

## Other higher powers

- **Chorus Without Mouths:** a distributed Aeon expressed unknowingly through
  unrelated creatures.
- **Last Surveyor:** a vanished ascendant whose instruments continue making regions
  perfectly legible, symmetrical, safe, and unable to support surprise.
- **Witnesses:** non-hostile figures produced when a revision becomes significant
  enough to require comparison between before and after.

## Minor entities

- **Remainders:** abandoned outcomes attempting to complete missing events.
- **Unnames:** creatures ordinary mobs cannot perceive because too many categories
  have been removed.
- **Surveyor Motes:** lights that measure builds and correct small imperfections.
- **Living Sections:** detached architectural surfaces with temporary anatomy.
- **Path Emanations:** creatures that exist only as moving routes.
- **Closure Hounds:** beings attracted to unresolved Contradictions that make hidden
  debts visible.
- **White Effigies:** inert representations of entities possible under other rules.
- **The Afterwards:** quiet figures remembering outcomes the world rejected.

# Post-Tier-6 Progression

Tier 6 does not lead to Tier 7. The player instead develops three living Manuscripts:

- **Manuscript of Relation:** Architectonics.
- **Manuscript of Definition:** Autogenesis.
- **Manuscript of Continuance:** Worldlines.

Each discipline passes through four qualitative states:

1. **Perception:** recognize and inspect hidden relations.
2. **Intervention:** perform bounded revisions.
3. **Authorship:** use Law Interpretations and accept Contradictions.
4. **Original Work:** invent or permanently personalize one rule.

Progress comes from proofs of understanding rather than experience points. Examples
include closing a folded space after another player uses it, returning matter consumed
by Reassembled Land, restoring an identity, accepting an unfavorable recorded outcome,
completing a missing cause, or resolving Contradictions from multiple disciplines.

## Contradiction expressions

All three costs use the existing Contradiction ledger rather than creating independent
resource bars.

- **Dislocation:** spatial revisions cause misplaced footsteps, disagreement between
  visible and actual position, incorrect door destinations, and folded trajectories.
  It closes through restoration of boundaries, borrowed terrain, and Fixed Points.
- **Dissonance:** identity revisions cause category leakage, shared animations,
  unwanted inheritance, and ambiguous names or allegiance. It closes through
  restoration, consent, release, and acceptance of displaced traits.
- **Remainder:** continuity revisions leave sounds without causes, repeated motions,
  prior block states, and visible obligations. It closes by completing missing causes,
  revisiting paths, accepting consequences, or concluding branches.

# Cross-Discipline Works

## Architectonics and Autogenesis

- **Genius Loci:** give a structure identity and purpose so it recognizes residents,
  obstructs intruders, and repairs itself through conserved matter.
- **Pilgrim City:** author a structure as a slowly relocating creature.
- **Ecology of Form:** create terrain emanations adapted to geometric roles.
- **Bone of the World:** reveal terrain as functional anatomy.

## Architectonics and Worldlines

- **Returning Road:** recreate a recorded journey as a continuous path regardless of
  current distance.
- **Siege in One Step:** compress a recorded advance into one spatial event.
- **Deferred Architecture:** project a structure now and assemble its matter when a
  trigger occurs.
- **Room After Room:** cycle one room through several recorded layouts.

## Autogenesis and Worldlines

- **Ancestral Parliament:** call prior valid forms of a creature as temporary
  advisers or defenders.
- **The Self That Continued:** manifest the caster implied by a path not taken.
- **Inheritance of Consequence:** pass a stored effect or obligation to a compatible
  successor.
- **Merciful Revision:** restore structural form and identity without restoring lost
  health.

## Works of all three

- **Authored Demesne:** define a bounded space, its permitted identities, and one
  recurring event.
- **Possible Host:** build a body from local matter and recall a recorded pattern into
  it.
- **Revision Without Erasure:** reconstruct a prior bounded state from currently
  available matter while preserving knowledge and consequences.
- **First Original Work:** combine one Shape, Component, Law, and Closure principle
  into a personalized authored spell.

# Original Works

An Original Work provides personal expression without unrestricted scripting. It
contains:

- one established component;
- one established shape;
- one authored Law;
- one unusual trigger or boundary;
- one explicit conservation rule;
- one Closure condition;
- a player-chosen title and geometric signature.

Examples include:

- **Principle of the Hospitable Wall:** a sealed wall becomes adjacent to a recorded
  refuge when its injured author approaches, then closes after sheltering one subject.
- **The Ninth Definition of Wolves:** wolves count as one distributed guardian while
  defending a named place.
- **A Road Which Remembers Rain:** a recorded path appears as a causeway during storms
  and guides allies to its endpoint.

# Design Boundaries

- Do not turn the disciplines into exclusive factions.
- Do not add Tier 7 or replace authorship with ordinary stat progression.
- Do not introduce generic Gnosis ore or a repetitive material grind.
- Do not make unrestricted scripting a prerequisite for Original Works.
- Do not allow revision to duplicate matter, erase deaths, refund arbitrary resources,
  or bypass server protections without an explicit, narrow design.
- Do not let higher powers grant ascension. They embody propositions the player may
  study, oppose, correct, or outgrow.
- Do not let visual chaos replace meaning. Every distortion must correspond to a
  specific representational failure.
- Do not make all authored power punitive. Responsible Closure should be satisfying,
  legible, and capable of producing mastery.
- Preserve ordinary Mana and Artifice spells as useful tools and foundational grammar.

# Success criteria for the idea vault

The vault succeeds if future MnAGnosis features can draw from it while retaining:

- three distinct but interoperable post-Tier-6 play styles;
- a consistent relationship between mechanic, lore, cost, and visual language;
- meaningful use of existing Gravity, Living Land, True Damage, Law, Contradiction,
  tesseract, Truth, and Broker concepts;
- player authorship rather than service to a new faction or higher power;
- clear boundaries suitable for multiplayer and survival play;
- enough unexplored space for focused designs to surprise the player.
